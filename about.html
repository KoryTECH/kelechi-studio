About the studio

One room, both sides of the chair.

Most places in Lagos make you choose: the barbershop or the salon. We never understood why, so we built one room that does both.

Our story

Opened 2019, between a printing shop and a sweet store.

Kelechi Studio started with one chair, one mirror and a barber who was tired of sending his sister down the road to get her hair done. So we put a styling chair next to the barber chair — same wall, same playlist, same room.

Six years on, it’s still the same idea: you shouldn’t have to split your Saturdays between two shops. If your partner wants a trim and you want your brows done, you sit together. If you want a fade this month and braids next month, that’s a normal sentence here.

No “his and hers” pricing. No pretending one side of the family isn’t welcome. Just chairs, mirrors and people who take their time.

House rules

Small rules that keep regulars regular.

  1. We quote before we start. Colour, braids, treatments — you hear the price before a drop of product touches you.
  2. The chair is yours. Say what you want, change your mind halfway, bring a reference photo. Zero judgement.
  3. Tools get cleaned between every client. No exceptions, no “it’s fine”.
  4. If it’ll ruin your hair, we’ll tell you. Even if it means we sell you less that day.
  5. Late is not a personality. We start on time and we respect your time.

Prefer to just show up?

Fridays and Saturdays are walk-in days. Come as you are — worst case, you wait a chair and drink some water.
